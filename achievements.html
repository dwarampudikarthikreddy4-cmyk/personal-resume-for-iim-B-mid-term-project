SAT 1430(800/800-MATHS;630/800-READING) 2025

Preparing for the SAT was a bit of a reality check for me, honestly. Maths felt super natural, so getting 800 there was not really a surprise, but the reading section was a whole different story. I used to struggle a lot with those long passages and tricky vocabulary, and there were days when it felt like I was just stuck. Still, I kept at it, practiced more, and slowly got better. That score is a mix of my strengths and my effort, and I think that’s what makes it feel real. It showed me that even the things I’m weak at can improve if I stay patient and keep working, even when it gets annoying.

IELTS 7.5 Band 2026

I got a 7.5 in IELTS while also preparing for my BBA entrance exams, and honestly it worked out pretty well because both needed similar skills. Reading, writing, and time management were the common parts, so I tried to make my practice more efficient instead of treating everything separately. It wasn’t easy, but having a routine helped a lot, and I think that’s what made the difference. There were definitely days when I felt overloaded, but I kept going, and it paid off. It felt good to see that the hard work was translating into something useful, not just random studying for the sake of it.
